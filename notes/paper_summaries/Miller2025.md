# Miller, T.; Mikiciuk, G.; Durlik, I.; Mikiciuk, M.; Łobodzi´nska, A.; ´Snieg, (2025) - The IoT and AI in Agriculture: The Time Is Now—A Systematic Review of Smart Sensing Technologies

**Summary:**

This paper presents a systematic review of smart sensing technologies integrated with IoT and AI in agriculture, focusing on current applications, challenges, and future directions. The authors discuss how these technologies contribute to precision farming, resource optimization, and automated monitoring of various agricultural parameters. They categorize smart sensing applications across different agricultural domains, including crop monitoring, livestock management, soil analysis, and environmental control. The review highlights the benefits of IoT and AI in enhancing agricultural efficiency, sustainability, and productivity.

The paper also delves into the challenges associated with the widespread adoption of these technologies, such as data management, interoperability, security, and privacy concerns. It emphasizes the need for robust infrastructure, advanced data analytics, and effective decision-support systems to fully leverage the potential of IoT and AI in agriculture. The authors explore various AI techniques, including machine learning and deep learning, used for data analysis, pattern recognition, and predictive modeling in agricultural contexts.

Furthermore, the review identifies emerging trends and future research directions, such as the development of more sophisticated sensors, integration of blockchain for data security, and the use of edge computing for real-time data processing. The paper underscores the importance of interdisciplinary collaboration among researchers, engineers, and agricultural experts to overcome existing hurdles and drive innovation in smart agriculture. The findings aim to provide a comprehensive overview for stakeholders and guide future research efforts in this rapidly evolving field.

**Key Contributions:**

*   **Systematic Review:** Comprehensive review of smart sensing technologies, IoT, and AI applications in agriculture.
*   **Application Categorization:** Classification of smart sensing applications across various agricultural domains.
*   **Challenge Identification:** Discussion of key challenges including data management, interoperability, security, and privacy.
*   **Future Directions:** Identification of emerging trends and future research avenues in smart agriculture.
*   **Interdisciplinary Collaboration:** Emphasis on the need for collaborative efforts to advance the field.
